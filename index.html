Our Story

A Sanctuary By The Sacred Waters

Nestled along the serene banks of the Ganga in Rishikesh, Kaafe was born from a simple dream — to create a space where travelers, seekers, and wanderers could pause, breathe, and connect with the eternal flow of life.

Our cafe offers the widest panoramic view of the sacred river, where every sunset paints a new masterpiece across the Himalayan sky. Here, time slows down, conversations deepen, and every cup of chai becomes a meditation.

We believe in conscious hospitality — serving food that nourishes both body and soul, crafted with locally sourced ingredients and love.

Sunset Experience

Every Hour Tells A Story

From the first light of dawn to the last glow of dusk, every moment at Kaafe offers a unique experience shaped by the rhythm of the river.

6 AM - 10 AM

Golden Morning

Start your day with sunrise yoga views, fresh juices, and the gentle sound of temple bells echoing across the river.

12 PM - 4 PM

Peaceful Afternoon

Savor a leisurely lunch while watching rafters and kayakers navigate the rapids. Perfect for reading, writing, or simply being.

5 PM - 8 PM

Magical Sunset

Witness the legendary Rishikesh sunset as the sky transforms into a canvas of orange, pink, and gold reflected on the sacred waters.
